## Conference Report

Conference Report

The Centre for International Governance Innovation hosted its fourth conference on Digital Governance in China on November 17, 2025, in Hong Kong, collaborating with the Centre on Contemporary China and the World at the University of Hong Kong. For the first time, the conference was held as an in-person, public seminar to examine the evolving features of digital governance in mainland China and Hong Kong.
